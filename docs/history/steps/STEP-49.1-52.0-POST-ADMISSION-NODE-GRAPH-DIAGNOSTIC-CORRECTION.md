# Step 49.1–52.0 — post-admission node-graph diagnostic correction / 0.0.182

Physical 0.0.181 closed Step 48 at 4/4 in the same process used for Step 49. Step 49 Gate A therefore accepted the exact off-tree `NMainMenu` authority. Gate B then resolved the retained `/Game/RootSceneContainer`, proved `NGame.RootSceneContainer` was null, token-matched `set_RootSceneContainer` at `0x06001B9B`, and proved the setter is a four-instruction, zero-call, one-`NSceneContainer`-field trivial assignment. Gate C repaired the property successfully with zero context/native drift and invoked the one-shot frozen `RootSceneContainer.AddChild(NMainMenu)`.

The run failed only during post-`AddChild` verification. `RequireStartupLadderRootSceneContainerPropertyIdentity` called the historical Step-39 whole-NGame graph enumerator. Once the newly admitted menu completed its automatic lifecycle, the NGame graph exceeded that helper's original 256-node diagnostic ceiling, producing `Step 39.0 node-graph traversal exceeded 256 nodes.` Rendering remained frozen and the process ended normally. This is a launcher diagnostic-limit failure, not evidence that `AddChild` itself failed or that a forbidden runtime boundary was crossed.

0.0.182 keeps scope at Steps 48–52 and makes no new runtime authorization. Exact retained `RootSceneContainer` authority is now resolved from **immediate NGame children only**: exactly one direct child named `RootSceneContainer`, exact managed type `MegaCrit.Sts2.Core.Nodes.NSceneContainer`, `IsInsideTree=true`, exact direct parent NGame, and nullable property consistency. This removes the post-admission menu subtree from root-container identity verification entirely.

Step 49 Gate C also gains durable checkpoints immediately after `AddChild` returns, after `NMainMenu.IsInsideTree=true`, after exact-parent verification, and after observing the RootSceneContainer child count. If a later assertion fails, the device report will distinguish lifecycle execution from verification failure precisely.

Steps 50 and 52 no longer reuse the immutable historical Step-39 256-node graph helper for the expanded in-tree menu. They use a separate menu-only breadth-first diagnostic enumerator capped at 4,096 nodes, with a boundary-specific overflow diagnostic. The Step-39 helper and its 256-node historical behavior remain unchanged. This increases diagnostic capacity only; it does not broaden any managed/native/platform/Steam authorization.

Steps 48–52 otherwise retain their prior contracts. Step 48 remains the guarded off-tree authority; Step 49 remains one-shot frozen admission; Step 50 remains the short audited render pulse/refreeze; Step 51 remains non-invoking single-player frontier mapping; Step 52 remains the 1.5-second audited render residency/refreeze.
